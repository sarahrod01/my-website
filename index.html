    How to complete this profile
    • Part A becomes the shortlist card in the employer portal. Part B is the full profile the card opens.
    • Fill in Part B first, then write Part A from it. Everything on the card must also appear in Part B.
    • Card limits: Best suited to ≤ 60 characters · Recommendation one sentence ≤ 160 · Strengths and watch-outs ≤ 60 each.
    • Always complete all key facts, even when the answer is a gap (e.g. “0 yrs Australian aged care”). Every card needs at least one watch-out.
    • Mark facts: ✔ Verified by Nursiecare · ◐ Candidate-reported · ○ Pending. Click a marker again to clear it.
    • Do not add a photo, age, marital status, religion, health or children’s details.
